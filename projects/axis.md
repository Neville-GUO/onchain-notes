# Axis Robotics (AXIS)

> **State**: token live — claim open, Season 2 starting
> **Last verified**: 2026-10-23

## What it is

A network that rewards contributions of real robotics training data. Collected data is used to train embodied-AI models; contributors are scored for the data they provide.

## Token status

`AXIS` went live on **2026-10-21**. The claim portal opened on Base at 12:00 UTC the same day.

## Notes

Two things are easy to conflate here: claiming Season 1 (a one-time action against an already-finalised snapshot) and earning Season 2 (an ongoing programme). Season 1's snapshot was taken before TGE, so activity after the snapshot does not affect it. Season 2 is the only part still accruing.

Participation is free — signatures on Base, no capital required.

## Risk notes

- Season 2 terms are published after Season 1 closed. Read the current Season 2 documentation rather than assuming the Season 1 mechanics carried over.
- Robotics-data projects can have hardware or region requirements at the higher reward tiers. Verify before investing time.
- The expiry date is the trap: a wallet can hold a claimable balance for over a year and lose it for lack of a transaction.
