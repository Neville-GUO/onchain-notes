# TastyCo (TASTY)

> **State**: pool size confirmed — no conversion rate, product not launched
> **Last verified**: 2026-10-23

## What it is

A consumer-facing app with a token planned for late 2026. The current phase is a points and quest campaign rather than a live product.

## Token status

The project has confirmed a **$100,000 pool, equal to 5% of supply**. What has *not* been published is how points convert into that pool. A pool size without a conversion rule tells you the ceiling, not your share.

`TASTY` is planned for late 2026. In-app points are stated to continue past the campaign end and count toward the launch.

## Rules as written

- **A profile on the project site is required** for eligibility. Activities performed without a profile do not count.
- Quest campaign on TaskOn: `taskon.xyz/quest/918682343` — the campaign is a submission-and-ranking format, not a task checklist. Participants submit a referral link; rewards are distributed to a ranked set of participants.
- Campaign end: **2026-10-25**.

## Notes

TaskOn is not reachable from mainland China IPs — the site returns an explicit region block. A VPN is required to access the campaign page. Zealy (`zealy.io/cw/tastyco`) and the project site itself are reachable directly.

The project site's task surface renders via WebGL and requires a connected wallet before it displays anything, which is why there is no visible TaskOn link on the site. The campaign must be reached via its own URL.

## Risk notes

- A front-end inspection in October 2026 showed testnet configuration, a development Telegram bot handle, and an auto-KYC endpoint pointing at a third-party domain — production and test configuration mixed on a live surface. Treat this as a signal about engineering maturity.
- Campaign participation is free but the reward structure is a ranked lottery across an applicant pool, not a proportional distribution.
- The 5% pool with no conversion rule means the expected value cannot be estimated. Do free tasks only; do not spend on the product.
