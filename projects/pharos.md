# Pharos (PROS)

> **State**: token live — claim window closing
> **Last verified**: 2026-10-23

## What it is

An L1 with a modular architecture aimed at real-world asset and DeFi workloads. Mainnet went live 2026-04-28.

## Token status

`PROS` is live and listed on major venues including Coinbase, OKX, and Upbit. The distribution phase is over — what remains is the claim window for wallets that qualified.

## Rules as written

- Claim portal: `claim.pharos.xyz`
- The window closes **2026-10-25**. Unclaimed allocations return to the foundation.
- No grace period has been announced. A missed claim is a permanent loss, not a delay.

## Deadline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2026-10-25 | Claim portal closes. Unclaimed tokens returned to foundation |

## Notes

Eligibility came from a combination of testnet activity, staking, Discord participation, and OKX wallet usage — so a wallet may qualify without having deliberately farmed anything.

The claim requires connecting a wallet and signing. There is a small claim fee in PROS that is covered by the allocation itself rather than requiring an out-of-pocket purchase.

## Risk notes

- The testnet site was repurposed into a funnel page for a separate product surface, so old bookmarks no longer resolve to the faucet. Use the claim domain directly.
- As with any claim: enter the domain by hand. A claim page reached through a DM or a search ad is not the claim page.
