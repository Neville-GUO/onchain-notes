# AlloX (ALLOX)

> **State**: token confirmed, not launched — conversion rule written down
> **Last verified**: 2026-10-23

## What it is

A DeFi platform with an RWA angle, running a points and portfolio-tracking programme ahead of its token.

## Token status

Not launched. The project has stated in writing that **each point converts 1:1 into ALLOX at token launch**, with **35% of supply allocated to the community**. This is one of the stronger written commitments in the current cycle — the conversion is stated as a rule, not as a possibility.

Reportedly ~363,000 users have been confirmed as qualifying for the eventual distribution. Note the wording: confirmed eligibility is a statement about a list, not about a balance.

## Rules as written

- Every point converts 1:1 at launch
- Community allocation: 35% of supply
- TGE targeted late October to early November 2026

Free activities:

| Activity | Reward |
|---|---|
| Daily login | 500 points |
| Gems | ongoing |
| Portfolio construction | 250 points |
| Prove Your Portfolio | up to 350,000 points per season |

## What to avoid

**Volume League Season 2** — advertised as a $550,000 reward pool. Participation requires real trading volume, which means gas, fees, and slippage. The pool is shared across all qualifying participants. Treat volume programmes as paid products, not as airdrops.

Also note: the token **public sale** is a different thing from the airdrop. The sale required KYC and cost money. It is not the same as earning an allocation.

## Risk notes

- Marketing figures published by the project (total users, share of profitable portfolios) are promotional. Do not read them as expected returns.
- The 1:1 point conversion does not state a point supply, so the implied value is unknown until launch.
- Pre-market data for this asset has been internally inconsistent and should not be relied on.
