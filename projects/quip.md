# QUIP

> **State**: points only — no token commitment found in writing
> **Last verified**: 2026-10-23

## What it is

A post-quantum computing network. The premise is that current signature schemes are a long-term liability and that a chain designed around post-quantum primitives is the correct starting point rather than a later migration.

## Token status

No token has been announced. Vault points accrue for participation, but no allocation or conversion rule has been published. Treat this as *points only*.

## Rules as written

- Points accrue in a Vault
- **A post-quantum account must be created first** at `account.quip.network/connect`. Without it, points may not accrue against the correct identity — this is the single most likely way to lose weeks of activity here.
- The project has continued shipping (a firewall contract was deployed in October 2026), which is a positive signal for the team but says nothing about a token.

## Deadlines

None announced. No snapshot date, no TGE date.

## Notes

The interesting thing about QUIP is that the gating condition is not effort but setup order. Creating the account is a one-time action that takes a minute and is easy to postpone indefinitely, and postponing it means the work that follows may not count.

This is the same failure pattern documented in [lessons.md](../lessons.md): the task load is not the problem, the registration step is.

## Risk notes

- No written token commitment. The most likely outcome of the current phase is that points are a product metric.
- Post-quantum positioning is technically credible but commercially unproven; the audience is narrow.
